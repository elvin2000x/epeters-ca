Privacy, in plain words

  
    
    
  • Your photo is shrunk in your browser, sent once to Google's Gemini model to read your colouring, and never written to disk. We keep a one-way fingerprint of the file so a repeat upload does not cost a second analysis.
    • 
    
  • What we store: the profile (season, palettes, notes), your shopping key, and the time it was made. No name, no email, no account.
    • 
    
  • The extension reads product colours on the page in front of you and sends nothing about your browsing anywhere. It only fetches your profile from our server using your key.
    • 
    
  • Delete everything by emailing hello@elvinpeters.com with your shopping key.
