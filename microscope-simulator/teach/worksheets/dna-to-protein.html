Lesson plan

  1. Write the partner strand for CGCGAATTCGCG. Then read your partner strand from right to left. What do you notice?

    Open: DNA molecules/#dna

  2. Which element forms the DNA backbone together with sugar? How do you know from the explorer?

    Open: DNA, phosphorus lit molecules/#dna?el=P

  3. Template strand: TAC CGT AAA GGC ATT. Write the mRNA.

  4. Circle the start codon and the stop codon. How many amino acids are in the protein? What is the first one?

  5. Delete the first G of GGC in the template and transcribe again in groups of three. What changes?

  6. How many coding nucleotides does each chain need, counting three per amino acid plus a three-letter stop codon?
