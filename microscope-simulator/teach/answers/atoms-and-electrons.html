Answer key · Lesson 6

Atoms and electrons

Lesson plan Worksheet

  1. Complete the table.

    Proton: nucleus, 1+, about 1. Neutron: nucleus, 0, about 1. Electron: outside the nucleus, 1-, about 1/1800 of a proton. Sources: [1]

  2. How many protons and neutrons are in carbon-12 and in sodium-23?

    Carbon-12: 6 protons, 6 neutrons. Sodium-23: 11 protons, 12 neutrons. Sources: [2, 1]

  3. Write the electrons per shell.

    Hydrogen 1. Carbon 2, 4. Oxygen 2, 6. Sodium 2, 8, 1. Magnesium 2, 8, 2. Chlorine 2, 8, 7. Potassium 2, 8, 8, 1. Sources: [2]

  4. Draw shell diagrams for oxygen and sodium.

    Oxygen: nucleus with 8 protons, 2 electrons in the first shell and 6 in the second. Sodium: nucleus with 11 protons, then 2, 8 and 1 electrons in three shells. Sources: [2, 1]

  5. Lithium, sodium and potassium are all in group 1. What do their atoms have in common?

    Each has one electron in its outer shell. They also have low first ionization energies (Li 5.4, Na 5.1, K 4.3 eV). Sources: [2, 3]

  6. Name one strength and one limit of the shell model.

    Strength: it shows clearly how many electrons are in each shell, which links to the group pattern. Limit: real electrons do not travel in circles; the viewer calls it a simplified picture. Sources: [2]

Exit ticket. Chlorine has 17 protons. How many electrons does a neutral chlorine atom have, and how many are in its outer shell?

17 electrons, arranged 2, 8, 7, so 7 in the outer shell. Sources: [1, 2]
