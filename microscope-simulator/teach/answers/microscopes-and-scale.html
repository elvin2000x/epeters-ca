Answer key · Lesson 3

Microscopes and scale

Lesson plan Worksheet

  1. Fill in: 1 mm = ____ micrometres, and 1 micrometre = ____ nanometres.

    1 mm = 1000 micrometres; 1 micrometre = 1000 nanometres. Sources: [1]

  2. The eyepiece field number is 18 mm. Calculate the field of view for each objective.

    4x: 40x total, 4.5 mm, 4500 micrometres. 10x: 100x total, 1.8 mm, 1800 micrometres. 40x: 400x total, 0.45 mm, 450 micrometres. 100x: 1000x total, 0.18 mm, 180 micrometres. Sources: [2, 3, 1]

  3. At 400x, about what fraction of the field does the Paramecium fill? Use it to estimate its length. Compare with the field guide.

    Answers vary with the estimate. Example: about half of 450 micrometres is about 225 micrometres, which is inside the field guide range of 170 to 330 micrometres. (The explorer draws it 240 micrometres long.) Sources: [2, 4, 3]

  4. What is the difference between magnification and resolving power?

    Magnification enlarges how big an object looks. Resolving power is the ability to show two close structures as separate, which sets how much detail you can see. Sources: [5]

  5. The head of ATP synthase is about 9 nm across. A student light microscope resolves about 200 nm. Can it show the head? Explain.

    No. 9 nm is much smaller than the roughly 200 nm the light microscope can resolve, so it cannot be seen as a separate structure. Sources: [6, 5]

Exit ticket. With the 10x objective the field of view is 1.8 mm. A cell stretches across one tenth of the field. How long is it in micrometres?

1.8 mm / 10 = 0.18 mm = 180 micrometres. Sources: [2, 1]
