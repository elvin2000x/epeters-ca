Answer key · Lesson 4

The pond: who eats whom

Lesson plan Worksheet

  1. For each organism, write what it eats, or write 'makes food from light'.

    Paramecium: eats bacteria, yeast and algae. Amoeba: eats bacteria and algae. Euglena: makes food from light with chloroplasts, and can live on organic matter in the dark. Diatom: makes food from light (photosynthetic alga). Sources: [1, 2, 3, 4]

  2. Record the Paramecium count every 10 seconds on both slides.

    Counts vary from run to run. Expected pattern: the count on slide A rises as well-fed Paramecium divide, while slide B does not grow because there is no food to support division. Sources: [1, 5]

  3. One second in the pond is how much real time? How many seconds of pond time match one 6 hour Paramecium division?

    720 times faster, so 1 second = 720 seconds = 12 minutes. 6 hours = 360 minutes, and 360 / 12 = 30 seconds. Sources: [5, 1]

  4. Draw the pond food web. Arrows point from the food to the eater.

    Light to green alga, Euglena and diatom. Bacteria, yeast and green alga to Paramecium. Bacteria and green alga to Amoeba. Sources: [1, 2, 3, 4, 6]

  5. Write a dichotomous key that names all six slides: Paramecium, Amoeba, Euglena, bacteria, yeast, diatom.

    Keys vary. One working key: 1. Inside a glass shell? Yes: diatom. No: go to 2. 2. Green chloroplasts and one flagellum? Yes: Euglena. No: go to 3. 3. No fixed shape, moves with pseudopodia? Yes: Amoeba. No: go to 4. 4. Covered in cilia? Yes: Paramecium. No: go to 5. 5. Round cells that grow buds? Yes: yeast. No (rods, spheres or spirals): bacteria. Sources: [4, 3, 7, 8, 9, 10]

Exit ticket. Draw one food chain from the pond with at least three links, starting with light. Label which organism makes its own food.

Example: light to green alga (makes its own food by photosynthesis) to Paramecium. Or light to Euglena. Arrows go from food to eater. Sources: [1, 6, 3]
