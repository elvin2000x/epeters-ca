Resistor puzzles

A lesson shows the arithmetic. Then you set one resistor so the meter hits the target. Four choices. One of them is right.

Try the nine

1. One resistor

Current = voltage ÷ resistance.

12 V ÷ 30 Ω = 400 mA

2. In a row

Add the ohms, then divide. The blank takes its share of the voltage.

10 Ω + 20 Ω = 30 Ω

3. The wire splits

Each branch gets the full battery. The currents add. A pair combines first.

600 mA + 400 mA = 1000 mA
